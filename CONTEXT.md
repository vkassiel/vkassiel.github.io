# Personal Dev Landing

Static landing page presenting Vitor Kassiel as a software engineer to recruiters. One page, one goal: get the recruiter to read the CV and make contact. A blog may be added later.

## Language

**Profile**:
The single subject of the site — the developer being presented (Vitor Kassiel). Owns the headline identity: name, role, tagline, bio.
_Avoid_: User, account, person

**Recruiter**:
The intended visitor — someone evaluating the Profile for a job. The whole page is written for this reader.
_Avoid_: Visitor, client, lead

**Role**:
The Profile's professional title (e.g. "Engenheiro de software"). Distinct from a Position held at a company.
_Avoid_: Job, title

**Experience**:
One job the Profile held: company, role, period, and a few outcome bullets. The timeline is an ordered list of these.
_Avoid_: Job, position, work

**SkillGroup**:
A named cluster of technologies (e.g. Backend, Frontend, Cloud). Skills are shown grouped, never as one flat pile.
_Avoid_: Tag, category

**Project**:
A showcased piece of work with name, one-line description, link, and techs. Currently placeholder content.
_Avoid_: Repo, work, portfolio item

**SocialLink**:
An outbound contact channel — email (mailto), GitHub, LinkedIn. Rendered as icon links in Contact.
_Avoid_: Contact, url

**CTA**:
The primary action asked of the Recruiter: download the CV. Everything else is secondary.
_Avoid_: Button, action

**CV**:
The downloadable PDF résumé served at `/cv.pdf`. The Profile's authoritative long-form credential; the landing is its summary.
_Avoid_: Resume (in code), curriculo
